About

I'm a final-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Jerusalem College of Engineering, Chennai, currently holding a CGPA of 8.0/10. My interest sits at the intersection of Python programming, robotics, and the Internet of Things — I like building things that sense, decide, and act.

That interest has taken me from wiring an IR-sensor path-following robot on an Arduino Uno, to annotating a 3,000-image dataset and training a YOLO model for an industrial pantograph inspection project, to a summer program at India Space Lab covering drone technology, CanSat/CubeSat satellite systems, and remote sensing. A technology internship at the Airport Authority of India also gave me exposure to enterprise networking and secure infrastructure.

Alongside my technical work, I've taken on leadership roles in student bodies including the Institution of Engineers (India) and Rotaract Club, and I continue to build my foundation in Python, data analysis, and applied machine learning.
